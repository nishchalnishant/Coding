# 06. SDE 2 Practice Plan

← [Part 33](README.md) · [Study plan](../STUDY_PLAN.md)

This four-week add-on assumes the main DSA track is already underway. Adjust the cadence to the interview date and role. Keep a practice log of the prompt, time, what went well, and one next action.

| Week | Focus | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Coding fluency and core CS | 4 mixed timed coding sessions; review OS, networking, and databases; explain one production incident |
| 2 | Low-level design | 2 design exercises; sketch APIs and state transitions; discuss concurrency and tests |
| 3 | System design | 2 full 45-minute designs; practice estimates, data model, reliability, and one deep dive |
| 4 | Full-loop readiness | 2 coding mocks, 1 design mock, 1 behavioral mock; revisit weak spots and project story |

## Mock review rubric

Score each item 0 (missed), 1 (partial), or 2 (clear and complete):

- Clarified meaningful constraints
- Explained the approach and alternatives
- Produced correct, readable work
- Checked edge cases and complexity
- Made trade-offs explicit
- Responded constructively to hints or new constraints
- Finished with a concise summary

For design mocks, also score requirements, API/data model, architecture, bottleneck analysis, failure handling, and observability. For behavioral mocks, score specificity, personal ownership, outcome evidence, and reflection.

## Readiness checks

- Solve mixed medium DSA problems without relying on topic labels.
- Explain correctness and complexity, and test code with deliberate cases.
- Complete a design discussion while adapting to changing requirements.
- Discuss a real project at architecture, implementation, and operational levels.
- Give concise behavioral examples with clear personal contribution and outcomes.
- Confirm the company's current loop and the language/tools expected for the interview.
